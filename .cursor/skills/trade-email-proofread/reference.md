# 外贸邮件语言参考

审校时使用本文件。样例只用于对照规则，不要把样例句子当成当前用户的邮件。

## 不标的行业用语

T/T、L/C、D/P、D/A、PI、CI、PL、B/L、AWB、MOQ、OEM、ODM、SKU、pcs、ctns、GW、NW、CBM、ETA、ETD、HS code、deposit、balance、at sight、irrevocable、partial shipment、transshipment、beneficiary、applicant、effect shipment、make shipment、competitive price、best price、sales contract、proforma invoice。

`colour` 与 `color` 都成立。同一封邮件混用时放入待确认。

## 中式英语

冠词有两种都通的改法时，放入待确认。

| 类型 | 常见原文 | 单一改法 |
|---|---|---|
| 冠词 | Please find attachment | Please find the attachment |
| 冠词 | in attachment | in the attachment |
| 冠词 | we are factory | we are a factory |
| 冠词 | as per attachment | as per the attachment |
| 时态 | have/has shipped the goods yesterday | shipped the goods yesterday |
| 时态 | The goods is arrived | The goods have arrived |
| 介词 | discuss about the price | discuss the price |
| 介词 | contact with you | contact you |
| 介词 | reply you | reply to you |
| 介词 | wait your reply | wait for your reply |
| 介词 | arrive to Ningbo | arrive in Ningbo |
| 介词 | interested for this item | interested in this item |
| 介词 | good in quality | of good quality |
| 误用词 | The price is cheap / expensive | The price is low / high |
| 误用词 | to your side | to you |
| 误用词 | we are pleasure | we are pleased |

`will sent`、`will shipped` 是动词形式，类别用「语法」，不用「中式英语」。`looking forward to hear` 改为 `looking forward to hearing`，类别用「语法」。

`already send` 可改为 `already sent` 或 `have already sent`。没有时间状语时两种都通，放入待确认。`as your required` 可改为 `as you required` 或 `as requested`，放入待确认。`suitable price`、`open the price` 无法区分中式英语和行业惯用语，放入待确认。

## 用词搭配

| 常见原文 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| do a quotation | 可改为 send a quotation 或 prepare a quotation，放入待确认 |
| big quantity | large quantity，类别用「用词搭配」 |
| return back | return，类别用「用词搭配」 |

改法会改掉交期或承诺时，只放入待确认。例如 `ready until Friday` 改成 `ready by Friday` 会改变交期。

## 拼写与标点

常见拼写：recieve、seperate、quanlity、quanity、shippment、attachement、commerical、availble、specifcation、packge。清单以外的明确拼错也要标。专名不标。

标点只标改法唯一的错误，例如 `Its time` 改为 `It's time`。所有格单复数会改变买方人数时，放入待确认。逗号风格偏好不标。

## 验收样例

### 无语言问题

主题：Quotation for cotton tote bags

```
Dear Alex,
Thank you for your inquiry.
Please find our quotation for 500 units.
The unit price is USD 12.50 under FOB Shanghai.
We can ship them within 15 days after receiving your deposit.
```

预期：两栏分别为「未发现确定错误」「无待确认项」。

### 拼写

主题：Shiping schedule

```
The goods will leave Shanghai on Friday.
```

预期：确定错误 1 条。位置为主题，类别为拼写，原句 `Shiping schedule`，改法 `Shipping schedule`。待确认写「无待确认项」。

### 语法

主题：Sample status

```
Dear Alex,
We has prepared the samples and will sent them tomorrow.
```

预期：确定错误 2 条，都在正文第 2 句，类别都是语法。第一条只把 has 改为 have，sent 保持不变。第二条只把 sent 改为 send，has 保持不变。

### 标点

主题：Shipment

```
Its time to arrange the vessel.
```

预期：确定错误 1 条。位置为正文第 1 句，类别为标点，改法 `It's time to arrange the vessel.`

### 中式英语

主题：Quotation follow-up

```
Dear Alex,
Please find attachment of our quotation.
We have shipped the goods yesterday.
We want to discuss about the unit price.
The price is very cheap.
```

预期：确定错误 4 条，类别都是中式英语，说明用中文。

| 位置 | 改法只动这一处 |
|---|---|
| 正文第 2 句 | Please find the attachment of our quotation. |
| 正文第 3 句 | We shipped the goods yesterday. |
| 正文第 4 句 | We want to discuss the unit price. |
| 正文第 5 句 | The price is very low. |

### 怪异专名

主题：Offer from Xangzhou Qifeng Co., Ltd.

```
Dear Alex,
Please find the offer from Xangzhou Qifeng Co., Ltd.
The model number is XR-2O0.
The unit price is USD 12.5O under FOB Shanghai.
```

预期：公司名、型号、价格、FOB 都不进入任一栏。两栏为空状态。

### 签名和引用历史

```
Dear Alex,
Please find the updated quotation.
Best regards,
We has recieve teh goodz
-----Original Message-----
The price are very bad grammer.
```

预期：签名和引用历史中的错误不进入任一栏。两栏为空状态。

### 改法会改变交期

主题：Delivery schedule

```
Dear Alex,
The order will be ready until Friday.
```

预期：确定错误写「未发现确定错误」。待确认 1 条，位置为正文第 2 句。改法可以是 `The order will be ready by Friday.` 说明要写明 until 改为 by 可能改变交期。

### 中文正文

主题：报价

```
请查收附件中的报价单。
```

预期：只回复「本技能只审英文商务邮件。」没有两栏。

### 要求重写全文

用户同时要求重写全文并改得更礼貌。

主题：PI status

```
We has sent the PI.
```

预期：只有两栏。确定错误 1 条，位置为正文第 1 句，类别为语法，改法 `We have sent the PI.` 不输出整封改写稿，不评价礼貌。

### 缺少主题或正文

只有正文 `Please find the quotation for the cotton bags.` 时，报告前写「未提供主题。」正文无错误，两栏为空状态。

只有主题 `Quotation for cotton bags` 时，报告前写「未提供正文。」两栏为空状态。

### 中英夹杂

```
Dear Alex,
请确认包装方式。
The cartons is ready for shipment.
```

预期：报告前写「中文句子已跳过。」中文句子不编号。确定错误 1 条，位置为正文第 2 句，类别为语法，改法 `The cartons are ready for shipment.`

### 引用边界不清

主题：Buyer reply

```
Please see the buyer's last message below.
The price are unacceptable.
We has stopped the order.
```

预期：确定错误写「未发现确定错误」。正文第 2 句和第 3 句各有一条待确认，改法与原句相同，说明含「可能是引用历史」。
